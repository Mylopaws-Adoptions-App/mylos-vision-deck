Why two programmes

Two kinds of suffering. Two programmes to end them.

TodayThe years ahead
Short-term suffering

The animal in pain right now

Hit by a vehicle, a maggot-infested wound, a mother in obstructed labour. Without surgery, these animals die slowly on the street.

Project LifelineTrauma surgery, recovery and vaccination
Long-term suffering

The litters not yet born

A single unsterilised female can give birth to 12 to 18 pups a year, most of them into hunger, disease and traffic.

Project Lifeline · The model

Mylos treats. The rescuer owns the journey.

Mylos

Subsidised vet & caregiver

  • Triage and diagnostics
  • Surgery in our own OT
  • 10 days of post-op care
  • Transparent case record and bill
  • Funds 50% of treatment
Rescuer / animal champion

Owns the rescue

  • Catching and transport
  • Consent and decisions
  • Raises 50% by crowdfunding
  • Release, foster or adoption
  • The animal's long-term life and care
